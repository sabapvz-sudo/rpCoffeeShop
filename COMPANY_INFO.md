## 4. Roasting Philosophy & Sourcing Standards

1. **Direct Trade & Producer Relationships**:
   - We work directly with smallholder farming families and cooperatives in Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, Panama, and Brazil.
   - We pay an average of 45% above Fair Trade minimums to ensure living wages, regenerative soil practices, and clean mountain water processing.
2. **Small-Batch Iron-Drum Roasting**:
   - Roasted every morning before dawn on our vintage 15kg cast-iron drum roaster.
   - We profile every lot individually to maximize origin characteristics rather than mask beans under heavy roast smoke.
3. **Freshness Guarantee**:
   - Coffee beans served on bar are rested between 5 and 18 days post-roast for peak flavor development and sweetness.
   - Retail bags sold in-store or shipped online are roasted within 72 hours of purchase.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) for Chatbot Training

### Hours & Operations
**Q: What are your opening hours?**  
A: We are open 7 days a week, Monday through Sunday, from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

**Q: When is Happy Hour?**  
A: Our Happy Pour Hours run daily from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM, offering 15% off all manual pour-overs (V60, Kalita, Chemex).

**Q: Where are you located and where do I park?**  
A: We are located at 428 Artisan Way in the Downtown Historic Mill District (Coffee Quarter). There is free 2-hour parking behind our roastery and metered street parking out front.

### Dietary & Ingredients
**Q: Do you charge extra for Oat Milk or other plant-based milks?**  
A: No! We believe everyone deserves great coffee. We offer Oat Milk (Oatly Barista), Macadamia Milk, Almond Milk, and Pistachio Milk with zero surcharge.

**Q: Do you offer decaf coffee?**  
A: Yes! Any of our espresso beverages, flat whites, or lattes can be made with our Swiss Water Process Decaf, which is 99.9% caffeine-free and 100% chemical-free without sacrificing sweet chocolate and caramel notes.

**Q: Are your syrups made in-house?**  
A: Yes. Our Madagascar vanilla syrup is simmered with real organic bourbon vanilla beans, our caramel is slow-cooked with fleur de sel sea salt, and we use raw local wildflower honey and organic lavender flowers.

**Q: Do you have vegan options?**  
A: Almost our entire drink menu can be made 100% vegan by choosing any of our plant milks (Oat, Macadamia, Almond, Pistachio). The only non-vegan menu items are the Affogato (contains dairy gelato) and drinks sweetened with honey (unless swapped for vanilla or muscovado syrup).

**Q: Are there nuts in your café?**  
A: Yes. We use almond, macadamia, and pistachio milks, and our Hazelnut Mocha Royale contains real roasted Piedmont hazelnuts. Guests with severe nut allergies should notify their barista so clean portafilters and sanitized pitchers are used.

### Remote Work & Atmosphere
**Q: Can I bring my laptop and work from your café?**  
A: Absolutely! We have free Gigabit fiber Wi-Fi (`VelvetBean-Guest`), power outlets at every booth, and a mezzanine focus nook for quiet working.

**Q: Are dogs allowed?**  
A: Well-behaved, leashed dogs are warmly welcomed on our outdoor garden patio. We have fresh water bowls and house-made peanut butter dog treats.

### Beans, Brewing & Subscriptions
**Q: Can you grind coffee beans for my specific brewer at home?**  
A: Yes. When you purchase any bag of whole bean coffee, our baristas will grind it to your exact brewer specification (French Press, V60, Chemex, Aeropress, Moka Pot, or Espresso) on our precision Mahlkönig grinder at no extra charge.

**Q: How do your coffee subscriptions work?**  
A: You can set up a recurring delivery (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly) through our website or app, saving 10% on every bag with free shipping on orders over $30. You can pause or cancel at any time.

**Q: Can I book a coffee tasting or workshop?**  
A: Yes! You can reserve our 45-minute Guided Cupping Flight ($15) or our Thursday evening Latte Art & Extraction Workshop ($35) online via our app or by speaking with a barista at the counter.
